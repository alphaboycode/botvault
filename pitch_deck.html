The Problem in Native Asset DeFi

Emerging Layer-1 ecosystems like BOT face severe friction and security vectors when utilizing native assets in modern decentralized finance.

⚡

UX Friction & Approvals

Users must wrap BOT to WBOT, approve allowances, deposit, and unwrap. Each extra transaction adds gas fees, latency, and drop-off rates.

🛡️

Inflation & Donation Attacks

Empty or low-liquidity ERC-4626 vaults without decimal offsets can have share prices manipulated by donation attacks, stealing victim deposits.

🧩

Composability Deficit

Without a standardized ERC-4626 yield/share token, lending protocols, DEXs, and structured yield aggregators cannot easily integrate native BOT.
