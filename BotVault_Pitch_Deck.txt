--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Native Token DeFi Friction:
   - Native network tokens (BOT) lack standard ERC-20 composability.
   - Users are forced into tedious multi-step UX: Wrap BOT -> Approve WBOT ->
     Deposit -> Monitor -> Approve -> Withdraw -> Unwrap.

2. Lack of Standardization:
   - Fragmented vault implementations create security risks and break protocol
     interoperability across DEXs and money markets.

3. The ERC-4626 Inflation Attack:
   - Newly initialized vaults without virtual shares are vulnerable to
     front-running donation attacks where early depositors lose funds due to
     share price rounding manipulation.

4. Reentrancy Vulnerabilities:
   - Native asset transfers (`call{value: ...}`) introduce critical reentrancy
     vectors unless strictly isolated with checks-effects-interactions and guards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3: THE SOLUTION - BOTVAULT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BotVault delivers a secure, battle-tested, and developer-friendly vault primitive:

- 1-Click Native Deposit & Redemption:
  Users deposit native BOT and get vBOT shares instantly. No prior ERC-20
  token approval needed, saving transaction costs and eliminating user UX friction.

- OpenZeppelin 5.x Audited Foundations:
  Built upon OpenZeppelin ERC4626, ERC20, and ReentrancyGuard contracts.

- Permanent Inflation Protection:
  Engineered with an overridden `_decimalsOffset() = 6`. Virtual shares and
  assets make donation/inflation attacks mathematically unprofitable and secure
  early liquidity providers.

- Strict Protocol Invariants:
  Underlying WBOT reserves are strictly locked and verifiable 1:1 against total
  vault assets at all times.

SLIDE 4: ARCHITECTURE & TECHNICAL INNOVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dual-Contract System Architecture:

      [ User / Protocol ]
              |          ^
  depositNative()      redeemNative()
              v          |
     +--------------------------+
     |         BotVault         |  <--- ERC-4626 Standard
     |  (Shares: vBOT, Dec: 24) |  <--- Decimals Offset: 6 (Virtual Shares)
     |  ReentrancyGuard (OZ 5)  |  <--- Checks-Effects-Interactions
     +--------------------------+
              |          ^
       deposit()        withdraw()
              v          |
     +--------------------------+
     |           WBOT           |  <--- WETH9-Style Wrapper
     |  (Asset: WBOT, Dec: 18)  |  <--- 1 WBOT = 1 BOT Native
     +--------------------------+

Key Technical Specs:
- Asset: WBOT (18 decimals)
- Vault Shares: vBOT (24 decimals: 18 asset decimals + 6 offset decimals)
- Pricing Math:
    shares = assets * (totalSupply + 10^6) / (totalAssets + 1)
- Testing Rigor:
    Passed 128,000+ invariant fuzz testing calls in Foundry with zero reverts
    or state violations.
